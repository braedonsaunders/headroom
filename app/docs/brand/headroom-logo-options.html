Seven directions for the Headroom mark

Each option is a mark, a wordmark lockup, an app icon and a full launch splash that keeps animating while the engine loads, then gets out of the way. They follow the same rules as Spoolyard, OpenBooks and Bunkhouse: one self-contained SVG, CSS-only motion, the resting frame is the complete logo (intros use fill-mode: backwards), and prefers-reduced-motion shows it static.
